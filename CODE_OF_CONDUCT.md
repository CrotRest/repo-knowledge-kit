# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project environment for contributors, users, and maintainers.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying precise about technical tradeoffs.
- Keep issue and pull request discussion focused on the project.
- Accept maintainer decisions when they are made to protect project scope, quality, or safety.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeated off-topic comments after maintainers ask for focus.
- Abusive or discriminatory language.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, close issues, block users, or take other reasonable action to protect the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the project maintainer through a private channel when possible.
